Privacy Policy for FlowForge

Last updated: August 28, 2026

FlowForge is a personal, self-hosted auto-scheduling application. This Privacy Policy describes how FlowForge accesses, uses, and stores your data when you connect third-party accounts, including Google Calendar.

1. Information We Access

When you connect your Google Calendar account, FlowForge requests read-only access (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly) to:

  • View calendar metadata (calendar names, colors, identifiers).
  • View your scheduled calendar events and busy intervals to fit auto-scheduled tasks around your real-world calendar commitments.
  • Read your account email address to display linked accounts in your self-hosted settings.

2. How We Use and Store Data

  • Self-Hosted & Private: All calendar event data and OAuth tokens are stored exclusively on your own self-hosted FlowForge database instance.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: FlowForge does not transmit, sell, share, or disclose your calendar data, personal info, or tokens to any external servers or third parties.
  • Read-Only Access: FlowForge only reads your schedule and does not modify, create, or delete events on your Google Calendar.

3. Revoking Access

You can disconnect your Google account at any time from the FlowForge Settings page or by revoking access in your Google Account Security settings.
